Active Learning

  • Two unavoidable facts about teaching: active learning is better, and technology is ubiquitous. Now combine those with the underlying principle that the construction of teaching and learning materials has a strong influence on how the material is taught. The conclusion is that we desperately need textbooks that beyond just supporting active learning, actually induce active learning, and those textbooks should be seamlessly integrated with technology that further supports active learning.

    --David Farmer, American Institute of Mathematics
